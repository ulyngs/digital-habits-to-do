Get back to that thing you meant to do. Digital Habits: To-Do keeps you on track with simple, beautiful task lists and powerful features built for distraction-free productivity:

- Focus Mode: pop out your task in a floating window, complete with a timer
- Minimalistic to-do lists: use tabs to manage multiple lists (e.g. Work, Personal) of tasks organised by context
- Stay on track: assign time to tasks and see time spent on tasks in a collapsible history
- Built-in integrations with your tools: sync tasks two-way with Apple Reminders and Basecamp, or build custom integrations
- Planning and calendar overview: built-in calendar view of tasks and plans, with calendar integration
- Favourites view: pin important tasks and see them in a dedicated list across all tabs
- Data backup and restore: export and import your data for backup or moving to another machine

Digital Habits: To-Do is open-source and collects zero user data.
Source code: https://github.com/ulyngs/digital-habits-to-do
